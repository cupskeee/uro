# Halcyon Deep

The year is 2091. Tethys Marine pays triple wages to anyone who will live three kilometres under
the Pacific on Halcyon Station, mapping the Kaiko Ridge for the metals the surface ran out of.
Six modules, forty crew, a supply sub every eight days, and HALE — the operations AI that runs the
air, the power and the doors.

Four days ago a saturation diver named Teo Varga locked out through the Moon Pool on a routine
survey and did not come back. The company calls it an accident. The divers' union calls it the
third in a year. And someone has deleted three hours of the airlock logs.

You are Sam Reyes, an insurance investigator sent down on the last supply sub with a contract to
find out what happened before the company's lawyers do. Nobody asked for you. Everybody is polite.
