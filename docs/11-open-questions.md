**OQ-2 · Pipeline shape** *(from: Generation Pipeline feedback — "not a fixed flow")*
Is plan→mechanics→generate→extract the right decomposition? Should extraction run inline (latency) or async post-commit (risk: player sees prose whose facts fail validation)? Do we need a distinct "director" pass for pacing/foreshadowing across beats?
*For now:* the `05` graph, extraction inline; instrument stage latency from Phase 0 and let data argue.
*Narrowed (D-90, 2026-11-03):* the inline-vs-async half is settled — extraction stays inline. The player waits on the work before the story (~7 s), which background extraction can't shorten; the extraction tail after it (p50 5.2 s, p90 10 s) ended before a reader got through the narration on 143 of 144 beats (a modelled 250 wpm). The director-pass half stays open. *Pacing measured (2026-11-03, docs/16 "the v0.15.0 long run"):* across three 146-beat runs no plot thread resolved; by a judge's labels some live thread had gone 25+ beats untouched on 54-102 of the 146 beats (the longest gaps 105 and 125 beats), a player's errand was dropped, and 55-62% of beats committed no new state; there were no near-repeat beats, and planted details came back unprompted (9-10 of 12 since D-78). *Narrowed (D-95, 2026-11-03):* a pacing director is built — once a live thread has gone 25 beats without coming up (the extractor now reports which known threads a narration developed), the next beat names it beside the intent, at most once every 10 beats; live, the narrator brought a named plot back 44/80 against 0/80 without, never overriding the player. Still open: closing plots (no thread resolved in 518 beats, with or without it), thread kinds or weights, and party-aware pacing.

**OQ-3 · Recall selection** *(from: Retrieval and Memory feedback; the field's known-hard problem)*
Entity-triggered + top-k + recency will miss thematic callbacks ("the promise made in beat 12"). Candidates: planner-directed retrieval (planner asks questions, memory answers), periodic "salience" re-scoring, per-thread memory indices.
*For now:* the `04` heuristic; collect failure cases in a recall-misses log during Phases 1–3.
*Narrowed (D-75, 2026-11-01), still open:* measured on a callback benchmark — 7 facts planted in a live session, each recalled by a named, a thematic and an oblique intent, ranked among 172 played beat memories — semantic recall finds 18/21 narration-only and 20/21 with the player's intent embedded in each memory (now built), and the engine no longer loses memory slots to beats still in the recency window. Planner-directed retrieval (recall runs before the planner, and no-ruleset play has none), query expansion, salience re-scoring, separate intent/claim/per-thread indices and a larger k were measured or reasoned out as not needed now. Open: callbacks that share no words or concepts with the original beat, corpora much larger than 172 memories, and whether the narrator USES what recall brings back. *The long run (2026-11-02, docs/16):* in a 146-beat campaign the planted fact reached the narrator in 12/12 callbacks 56-122 beats later (top-4 ranks among 70-125 memories, or through claims), but the narrator used it in 8/12 — once ignoring an active thread that stated the errand. Retrieval holds at campaign scale; narrator adherence is the open half. *Narrowed (D-78, 2026-11-02):* with what recall brings back moved beside the intent under a header that asks for the specific detail, the narrator used the planted fact in 53/60 design samples against 35/60, and 21/25 against 16/25 on fresh pairs (live, one model). Still open: adherence is policy, not structure (a callback the data can't link — the letter's carter→Hesk — stays unmet), the small rise in unstated player acts on ordinary beats, and old facts the narration brings back being re-recorded in other words (cut from 14 to 2 of 60 callbacks by D-82). *Role nouns (D-86):* recall still puts an actor on stage only by name or alias — a 'the <role>' rule would have been wrong 7 of 11 times on the two long runs; aliases are the precision-controlled link. *The v0.15.0 long run (2026-11-03, docs/16):* 9/12 callbacks used, 0 `no`; of the 10 plants recall brought back, 9 were used in full, and the two misses were retrieval on lines that name neither the person nor the thing (5th of 78 with 4 returned, 13th of 130) — the open case above, whose rank moves with each run's wording.

**OQ-4 · Off-screen simulation depth & belief diffusion** *(Actor engine)*
Simulate-on-observation is the MVP answer, but: how deep (agenda-step vs. full beat-sim)? Do faction-level agendas tick on world-time boundaries even unobserved (wars shouldn't freeze because nobody watched)? What stops interim-outcome generation from contradicting a *different* place's interim outcomes? And — the part the war-story test (`10` Phase 5, `15C`) leans on — how does a belief *spread* NPC-to-NPC so a tavern regular two regions away can retell a rumor, with distortion?
*For now:* T3 actors resolve on observation; faction agendas advance only at downtime/time-skip boundaries; **belief fan-out is tier-agnostic** — on observation, an actor may acquire beliefs circulating among its contact edges (`BeliefChanged`, emitter A), confidence/detail decaying per hop, so an ordinary T0–T1 NPC can carry a rumor. Diffusion depth/rate is a placeholder to tune with play data. (Scheduled as a Phase 5 deliverable, `10`.)
*Narrowed (D-76, 2026-11-01), still open:* measured on every session played on the instance — the clock moved once (one manual time-skip; furthest day 2), ~3 of 209 distinct narrations pass time, 7 reaction-rule rumors and 3 agenda belief spreads ever, 10 `knows` edges in all and none from play. So depth isn't the bottleneck: the deterministic machinery barely runs. Settled: off-screen depth is a deterministic agenda step at explicit time-skips (no beat-sim, no LLM interim outcomes: agenda outcomes are authored data, so a contradiction between places can only be the pack's own — not checked); diffusion is `propagate_belief` over an authored (`knows` on actor seeds) or module-added graph, distortion as confidence decay. Ashfall now exercises it (`test_the_world_moved_while_you_were_away`). Open, each with a named gate in D-76: a clock that follows play, per-event time stamps and time in prompts, bystanders overhearing rumors in a scene, contact graphs derived from co-location, and extractor-proposed `knows` edges (refused). *The long run (2026-11-02, docs/16):* the narration passed time on every beat where the player did (8/8) and almost never otherwise (1/138, player-driven); the fiction ran ~36 days to the clock's 28. Bystanders "overheard" in play: the narrator improvised it from recall and the extractor recorded it, with no harm observed. *Settled (D-87, 2026-11-03):* no engine pass gives an overheard rumor to anyone and no `knows` edge is derived from shared beats or places — a mention-based presence signal was right ~10% of the time, and nothing mechanical reads a belief. *The clock (D-77, 2026-11-02):* it now follows solo play, read from the player's intent (no LLM); party play, segments, time in prompts and per-event stamps stay open with D-77's gates.

*Trust model HARDENED (Phase 8 → D-32), but the OQ stays OPEN:* `distill_outcome` is now trust-scoped — a bundle can't kill/loot/first-hand-witness a PC, a T2+ named actor, or a bystander outside its declared cast (protected/out-of-cast casualties downgrade to `truth=unknown` testimony); loot needs real ownership; feat.actor is entity-resolved; bundles are size-capped and replay-idempotent. An adversarial audit's 25 exploits (incl. an authed POST killing/looting arbitrary actors) are closed. STILL OPEN (the "full contract", deferred to a real external game): a persisted **parked-encounter registry** (Uro pre-declaring the authorized roster/nonce → non-self-attested scope), **outcome-bundle schema beyond v1** (v1 is `extra='forbid'`+pinned, D-41), **time mapping** (game clock ↔ `world_time`), game-declared feat thresholds, witness reporting obligations, and finer endpoint→campaign authority.

*Narrowed (D-89, 2026-11-03):* closed-tier provenance is now checked where events are committed and imported — an event stamped `external` must be one distillation can make, and one stamped `module` one the rules gauntlet can make (Seventh Vault G-27).
